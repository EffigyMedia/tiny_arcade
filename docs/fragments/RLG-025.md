### RLG-025 — Five driving features are built but never played through
- Description: Five things exist in the code and were confirmed by probe rather than by play: the
  full tournament ladder in both classes to gold; the Cruiser and Super Cruiser unlock conditions
  firing; a speed trap that triggers in normal play; a super cruiser that deploys and gives chase;
  and lap counting over a real five-lap race.
- Intent: A probe proves that a code path exists. It does not prove that a player reaches it. Each
  of these five is a reward or a threat that the game promises, and a reward that never fires is
  indistinguishable from a broken one.
- The cheapest route for most of them is the harness: the drive test already drives with an
  autopilot, and a longer run with a scripted goal reaches the unlock conditions and the speed trap.
  The tournament ladder needs a player.
- Done when: each of the five is observed, and the ones that do not fire become their own defects.
