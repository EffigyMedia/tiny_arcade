### RLG-055 — A thumb lands where it lands: touch reaches past the canvas

- Description: Thirteen cabinets bound touch to their canvas rather than to the page, against the
  design document's own interaction principle - *"a thumb lands where it lands… making someone hit
  a 360-pixel target to steer is a fight with the game rather than with its hazards."* Where the
  canvas is much squarer than a phone, that left large dead bands. Fixed in the four where the
  audit measured it as real harm; the rest are near-full-height and were left alone deliberately.
- **Coil** - a 306x378 board on a 390x844 phone leaves roughly 150 pixels dead above and below it.
  Swipes now come from `Arcade.gesture`, which reads the whole page and already ignores anything
  starting on a control or an open panel, and stops while the shell is paused.
- **Ribbit** - same shape, same fix, plus a tap anywhere is a hop forward, which is the move a
  player makes most.
- **Ziggurat** - page-wide, but it **cannot** use `Arcade.gesture`: that layer reports four
  CARDINAL directions and this game has no cardinal move at all, only the four diagonals, which is
  deliberately its whole difficulty. It keeps its own reading of the swipe and takes it from the
  page instead of the canvas, skipping controls by hand.
- **Feather is the one that mattered most.** Thrust was the bottom strip *of the glass*, and the
  glass is 320x440 logical inside a far taller phone - so the natural resting place for a thumb,
  the bottom of the SCREEN, sat below the canvas and did nothing. **In a game where thrust timing
  is the game, the most important control was in the least reachable place.** Zones are measured
  off the viewport now.
- Not changed, and this is a decision rather than an omission: Popshot, Ricochet, Soviet Blocks and
  the rest are near-full-height, so their canvas and their screen are close to the same thing.
  Converting them would be churn for no reachable gain.
- The frozen `DESIGN.md` claims `Arcade.gesture` is *"built, and every machine uses it."* That was
  never true; it is closer to true now, and the design document records the real state.
- Verified: 18/18 cabinets plus the launcher check.
