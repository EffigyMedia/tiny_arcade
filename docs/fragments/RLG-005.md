### RLG-005 — Sector times and a live delta
- Description: Split the lap in three, keep the best sector times, and show a live delta against
  them, for example "+0.31".
- Intent: This is the entire hook of a time trial, and it is arithmetic on data the game already
  holds. It also gives the player a reason to care about a corner that [[RLG-002]] is making matter.
- Done when: three sector boundaries exist on any generated circuit, the best sector times persist
  across a session, and the HUD shows a signed delta that updates as the lap runs.
- **RULED 2026-08-23T23:58:46-04:00** — BUILT - SEEDED FROM A STALE DOCUMENT AND ALREADY DONE. Verified in code on 2026-08-23: `SECTORS = 3`, `secBest`, `secIdx`, `secStart`, `secDelta` and `sectorOf(z)` are all in `games/sw/raceway.html`, and the HUD reads `LAP 1/5   FUEL 83%   M 93%   S2 +0.41`. The lap is cut in three by DISTANCE - real circuits place sector lines by feel, and by distance is honest and needs no authoring. Colour follows the convention every timing screen uses, because it is read at a glance and nobody has to be taught it: purple for the best that sector has ever been, green for better than your own best, yellow for worse. **One guard in it is worth not losing: a sector records only if it lasted more than half a second.** Without that, a lap starting mid-sector writes a fragment of a sector as a record and every delta after it is nonsense for the rest of the session. Confirmed live by `drive-test.py` on 2026-08-23: the HUD advanced S1 to S3 within a 30-second run.
