### RLG-040 — Keep a slot open for the next Effigy Original
- Description: There is no brief for the next Original. The standing rule is to keep a slot open in
  every other batch, so that the floor never becomes only clones.
- Intent: **The clones are the floor. The Originals are the reason for the arcade.** They are also
  the only machines that carry no legal exposure and the only ones the design document proposes to
  sell.
- Ideas parked before the standup, none of them a commitment: a tide-based puzzler where the board
  floods and drains on a fixed clock; something that uses the microphone; a one-button rhythm game
  on the existing synthesizer; and a courier game across a procedural city, scored on route choice
  rather than on reflex.
- Done when: a brief exists for one new Original, at which point it becomes its own fragment.
