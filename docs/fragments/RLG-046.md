### RLG-046 — Cap corner sweep at 90 degrees so the renderer is never asked for the impossible

- Description: A pseudo-3D road draws curvature by offsetting each slice sideways in screen pixels
  as it recedes. That works up to about a 90-degree turn. Past it the road runs off the side of the
  frame and there is nothing to draw, because a road that has turned 180 degrees is behind the
  camera. **The generator can still produce a corner that sweeps past the limit.**
- Intent: The clamp is one line and it stops the renderer being asked for something it cannot
  express. The circuits become road courses with fast sweepers and no true hairpins - which is most
  of Silverstone and none of Monaco. That is a design decision the frozen log already recommends:
  *"Option 1 is what I would ship."*
- The alternatives, recorded so nobody re-derives them: **rotate the camera with the car**, which is
  real 3D and a different renderer - every sprite needs a heading, the road becomes a mesh, and the
  whole projection goes; or **cut away at the apex** to an overhead shot, which is cheap and reads,
  and takes control away at the exact moment control matters. Both are the size of the rest of
  Raceway put together.
- Related: [[RLG-002]] clamps curvature to the tightest hairpin a league asks for, which is a
  different clamp - that one is about how hard a corner bites, this one is about how far it turns.
- Done when: no generated corner sweeps past the renderer's limit, and the generator's corner mix is
  re-checked after the clamp.
