### RLG-012 — Per-biome skyline art
- Description: The five biomes change the ground, the weather, and the light. **The skyline does
  not.** The desert still shows city towers.
- Intent: The biome system is otherwise complete and it is the largest single source of variety in
  both driving games. One wrong horizon undoes the illusion that the world changed.
- Done when: each of the five biomes draws its own horizon band, and a desert never shows a city.
- **RULED 2026-08-23T23:58:47-04:00** — BUILT - SEEDED FROM A STALE DOCUMENT AND ALREADY DONE. Verified in code on 2026-08-23: `buildSkyline()` in `road.js` generates a silhouette per biome - city towers with lit windows, desert mesas that are wide and flat-topped and far apart, mountain peaks as tall overlapping triangles, the same peaks on pale slate for tundra, and a forest treeline of narrow conifers with trunks. It is re-run whenever the biome changes, so Highway's cycle rebuilds the horizon as it crosses. **One plan structure carries all five, because a silhouette is a silhouette** - only the shape generator differs, and lit windows are a city idea suppressed everywhere else. The reason it mattered: what stands on the horizon is the strongest single signal of where you are, and it was the one thing that never changed.
