### RLG-013 — Surface the weather and compound options in the menu
- Description: `optWeather` and `compound` both exist in the code and neither has a control in the
  menu. The player cannot reach either one.
- Intent: Two built systems that no player can use. The work is interface, not mechanics.
- Depends on: [[RLG-011]] for where the controls go, and [[RLG-008]] for the compound choice to mean
  anything.
- Done when: weather and compound are set from the session screen, and the setting reaches the race.
