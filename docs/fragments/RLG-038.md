### RLG-038 — The Golden Era queue - nine machines still unbuilt
- Description: Fourteen Golden Era machines are on the floor. Nine remain in the queue: Myriapod
  (after Centipede), The Well (Tempest), Grid Riot (Robotron 2084), Lance (Joust), Fuse (Bomberman),
  Southpaw (Punch-Out), Plumb (Pipe Mania), Tilt (Marble Madness), and Horde (Gauntlet).
- Intent: This is one item so the shelf has one place to be counted from. **Each machine becomes its
  own fragment when it is scheduled**, and its full specification is already written in the frozen
  design notes, in the shape the process expects.
- The batch order, and why it is not arbitrary: three at a time, always mixed, so that no two
  consecutive builds feel the same. Grid Riot needs the twin-stick overlay ([[RLG-030]]) and
  possibly landscape ([[RLG-032]]). **Tilt and Horde are parked by decision until everything else is
  on the floor** - Tilt carries the most technical risk on the list, and Horde has to prove that it
  is not simply Derelict in real time.
- Every one of them obeys the clone posture in the design document section 3: our name, our palette,
  our typography, our sound, and a distinct accent.
- Done when: the shelf is complete, or an item here is explicitly declined.
