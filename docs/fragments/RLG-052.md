### RLG-052 — Arcade.menu - a cursor for every cabinet's own menus

- Description: Every cabinet's menu could only ever be answered with its FIRST button. The pattern
  copied into all eighteen binds Enter, and pad A, to `veilBody.querySelector('.btn').click()` -
  whichever button comes first. PLAY and PLAY AGAIN are first, so the play loop worked and hid it.
  **OPTIONS, CONTROLS and QUIT were pointer-only** - including the CONTROLS page that exists to
  tell a keyboard player which keys to use.
- **Built in the shell, not in eighteen files.** The fix is identical everywhere and the cabinets
  already agree on the shape: a container that becomes visible, holding buttons. `Arcade.menu`
  finds the deepest visible panel with more than one button, moves a cursor through them, and
  clicks the one it is on. Nothing is imposed on a cabinet's look: the cursor is one class,
  `.ark-cursor`, an outline in the machine's own accent, and a cabinet may restyle it.
- **Two collisions had to be handled, and both are the reason this is worth writing down.**
  - The keyboard is taken in the **capture phase** and stopped there. The games' own handler is
    still bound to "Enter clicks the first button" and would otherwise fire as well, so every
    choice would collapse back to the first item.
  - The pad needed the same. `A.pad.onPress` now lets a subscriber **swallow** a press by
    returning `true`, and the menu registers first. Every existing subscriber returns `undefined`,
    so nothing else changes.
- Arrow keys are claimed only while a menu is actually open, so gameplay steering is untouched.
- Verified by driving it: on Coil, Aegis and Soviet Blocks the cursor lands on PLAY, moves to
  OPTIONS, and Enter opens the Options panel rather than starting a game.
