### RLG-033 — An attract-loop kit
- Description: The cabinet animations in the launcher are hand-written per machine, in one `draw`
  map at the bottom of `index.html`.
- Intent: A small shared helper would stop the cards from drift out of step with the machines they
  advertise. Cards have gone stale three times: flat bars months after the machine became beveled
  solids, origami after the machine became machined hulls, and a white arrow after the player became
  a surveyor.
- Note: the rule that covers the same failure today is in `CLAUDE.md` - reskin a machine and update
  its card in the same unit of work. A kit is the structural version of that rule, not a replacement
  for it.
- Done when: the common card motions are a helper, and a new machine's card is a short declaration
  rather than a hand-written loop.
