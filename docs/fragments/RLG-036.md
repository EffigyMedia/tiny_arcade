### RLG-036 — A reward screen for the Super Cruiser unlock
- Description: Highway's unlocks each promise a reveal. The Cruiser reveal exists. **The Super
  Cruiser has none**, so the hardest unlock on the ladder - 20 miles at a 180 mph average - pays out
  in silence.
- Intent: An unlock with no reveal reads as a bug. The reward is the moment, not the vehicle.
- Depends on: [[RLG-025]], which is where it is confirmed that the unlock fires at all.
- Done when: the Super Cruiser unlock shows a reveal in the same register as the Cruiser's.
