### RLG-011 — Split the garage, and stop toggles from looking like actions
- Description: The menus are 29 buttons built by string concatenation into `openVeil(html, handlers)`
  across three screens. **The garage does eleven jobs on one screen**: car, paint, stripes, gearbox,
  mode, timed, pursuit, qualify, and drive. Nothing is grouped, and a mode toggle that reads
  "TIMED - ON" looks exactly like the button that starts the race.
- Intent: The car is the thing the player came to look at, and it currently shares a screen with a
  gearbox toggle. The fix is structural, not cosmetic.
- The shape of the work, from the frozen note: split the garage into a car-and-livery screen and a
  session-settings screen reached from it; put a switch with a state on a row with its label at the
  left and its state at the right; give each screen one primary action that is visually louder than
  everything else; and let a fork hide what it does not use, for example `CFG.garageHide:['chase']`,
  so Raceway stops offering a police chase it has no use for.
- Done when: the garage is two screens, every toggle reads as a toggle, and Raceway offers nothing
  that belongs only to Highway.
