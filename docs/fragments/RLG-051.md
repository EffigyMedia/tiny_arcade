### RLG-051 — Every input mode can play every cabinet

- Description: Three cabinets broke the design document's Must that no input mode is second-class.
- **Aegis could not be played at all on a keyboard or a gamepad.** Firing was a canvas
  `pointerdown` and nothing else; both other handlers served menus only. A player could open the
  cabinet and never take one shot. It now has a **driven crosshair** - arrows or the stick move it,
  space or A fires where it sits - which is how every console port of this game solved the same
  problem. The crosshair appears only once something drives it, so a touch player never sees a
  cursor they have no use for, and a real pointer puts it away again.
- **Vector's gamepad could fire and hyperspace but never turn or thrust.** Its 16 ms poll read the
  keyboard map only. It now reads the stick as an axis and falls back to the d-pad, exactly as
  Feather and Deep already did. `a` became thrust, so fire moved to the shoulder and X.
- **Burrow's pad TOGGLED the pump** where touch and the keyboard both hold it. Holding is the whole
  cost of the mechanic - let go and it deflates - and a toggle handed pad players that risk for one
  tap. It is held in the existing poll now.
- Verified: 18/18 cabinets and the launcher check; the driving pair still 21/21.
