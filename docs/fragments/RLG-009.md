### RLG-009 — Flags and lapped traffic
- Description: Yellow flags where a car is wrecked, blue when the leader is catching you, and the
  chequered flag at the end. Lapped traffic is not identified as lapped.
- Intent: Both are cheap, and both make a race feel officiated rather than simulated. A race with no
  officials is a time trial with company.
- Done when: a wreck raises a yellow in its sector, a car about to be lapped is shown a blue, and
  the race ends under a chequered flag.
