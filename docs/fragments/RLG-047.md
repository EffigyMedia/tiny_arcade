### RLG-047 — Tunnels, as a z-range effect rather than a second ribbon

- Description: A tunnel is a z-range with a roof and a light change. An overpass seen from
  underneath is the same trick. **Neither needs the renderer to draw two roads at once.**
- Intent: It is the cheap half of what [[RLG-016]] wants, and it can ship first. `drawRoad` walks
  one ribbon indexed by z, so a genuine crossing - where you can see the road you will be on in two
  minutes - needs the road to become a list of visible segments, each projected and depth-sorted.
  That is a rewrite of the thing everything else sits on. A tunnel is not.
- Done when: a generated circuit can carry a tunnel, with a roof, a light change on entry and exit,
  and the engine note changing inside it.
