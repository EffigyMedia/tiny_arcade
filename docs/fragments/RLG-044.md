### RLG-044 — drive-test should report brake share and slowest corner

- Description: `tools/drive-test.py` asserts that speed rises above 150 mph, that the car stays on
  the road, that laps count and that fuel and tires burn. **It does not report the two numbers that
  say whether a circuit drives like a circuit**: brake time as a share of the lap, and the speed at
  the slowest corner.
- Intent: Those two numbers are the acceptance test for [[RLG-002]], and [[RLG-002]] is the item
  that decides whether Raceway is a racing game. The corner fix is built and its result is recorded
  - CUP 99 mph and 6.9%, GT 102 mph and 10.1%, GP 123 mph and 7.7% - **but that measurement lives in
  a frozen document and cannot be repeated.** The design document names 15% braking as a target, and
  nothing in the repository can currently tell you whether the game still meets it.
- **This is what the frozen START-HERE meant by "the harness is the measuring stick."** It named the
  capability as something the harness could do, not something it does.
- Worth knowing before it is built: the autopilot influences both numbers. A driver that saws the
  wheel wears tires four times faster and brakes differently, and that has already produced one
  wrong conclusion in this project. Report the driver's own inputs beside the result.
- Done when: a drive-test run prints brake time as a share of the lap and the slowest cornering
  speed, per league, and the design document's performance table can be checked from a command.
