### RLG-014 — A championship across circuits
- Description: A season of races across several circuits, with points carried between them. Highway
  has a four-round tournament with points and medals; Raceway has nothing above one race.
- Intent: A circuit racer without a season is a collection of single races. The tournament ladder
  that Highway already runs is the model, and the pieces it needs from Raceway are the grid
  ([[RLG-004]]) and the end card ([[RLG-003]]).
- Done when: a championship runs a fixed set of circuits, carries points, and ends on a standing.
