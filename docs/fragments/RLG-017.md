### RLG-017 — A real Raceway wordmark
- Description: Raceway's title art is a holding palette. Highway has a drawn wordmark; Raceway has
  the seam that would carry one (`CFG.titleArt`, `logoCool`, `logoHot`) and nothing final in it.
- Intent: The two games share an engine and must not share a face. The seam exists, so this is art,
  not engineering.
- Done when: Raceway's title draws its own wordmark through the existing seam.
- **RULED 2026-08-23T23:59:14-04:00** — AMENDED 2026-08-23 after a code check. **The title SCENE was rebuilt and the wordmark was not.** Raceway's title is now the start grid seen from behind the last row - two staggered columns on the painted boxes, the gantry overhead running its five reds, packed grandstands, floodlight pylons - which is an image that could not be mistaken for Highway. But the comment at `games/sw/raceway.html:1660` says the wordmark is still Highway's warm chrome on a floodlit night scene, and `logoCool` / `logoHot` still carry a holding palette. So the seam works and the art through it is borrowed. **The two games share an engine and must not share a face.**
