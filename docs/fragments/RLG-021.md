### RLG-021 — Highway is an Original in the design notes and sits on the Second Wave shelf
- Description: The frozen design notes list Highway under **Effigy Originals** - ours outright,
  descended from nothing - and say that Raceway descends from Highway. The catalog gives both
  machines the `sw` shelf, which is the 1990s floor: the shelf for clean-room takes on 1990s
  cabinets.
- Why this is not cosmetic: **the commercial split drops a shelf.** The intended posture is to sell
  the Originals and to keep the clones free, and the mechanism is the shelf field. A machine that is
  an Original and is filed on a clone shelf is either dropped from a build it should be in, or kept
  in a build it should not be.
- The two readings, so the owner can rule rather than reconstruct: either the `sw` shelf grew into
  "the driving floor" and the design note is stale, or the two driving games are Originals that were
  filed by mechanism instead of by origin.
- This is the owner's call. Do not guess it.
- Done when: the shelf assignment and the design document agree, and the design document's Decision
  Log records which way and why.
