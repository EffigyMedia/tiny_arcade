### RLG-054 — Phalanx's title card draws this game's creatures, not the generic invader

- Description: The title card rendered an 8x4 rank of the **generic pixel space-invader** - the
  antennae, the two-frame leg shuffle, the whole icon - while the game itself has drawn its own
  medusa, crab and louse since it was built. The two shared nothing.
- Why it mattered more than a stale card: the design document's clone posture is a **hard
  constraint** and says it in as many words - *"no lifted sprite shapes, colour schemes, level
  layouts or character designs… where a mechanic needs a familiar read, express it in our own
  visual language."* The game does exactly that. **The title reverted to the borrowed icon**, on
  the front door of a cabinet standing on the shelf that carries this project's stated legal
  exposure. It also contradicted the `LICENSE` shipped at v0.9.2, which states that no sprite of
  any other party is reproduced.
- **Stated honestly in the audit and worth repeating: this was not a byte-for-byte copy of anyone's
  bitmap.** It was the unmistakable invader silhouette rather than this game's own designs - and on
  that shelf, that is the distinction that matters.
- Implementation: the rank is drawn from the same three body plans `creature()` uses in play, at
  title scale, in a 16-unit box scaled to the card. Medusa at the back, crabs through the middle
  ranks, lice at the front, each with the darker bone showing through that makes the in-game
  creature read as a specimen plate. **The composition is unchanged** - same eight-by-four grid,
  same fixed pattern of casualties, same sway - because the composition was good; only the animals
  were somebody else's.
- Verified: the creature band of the title canvas renders 8,801 lit pixels with no page errors, so
  the card is drawing rather than silently failing.
