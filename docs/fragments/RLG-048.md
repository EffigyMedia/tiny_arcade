### RLG-048 — Finish tuning the billboard car angles

- Description: Cars ahead are drawn rear-on when pointing away, three-quarter as they turn in, and
  in profile through the corner. The system is **built and honestly half-tuned** - the frozen log
  records two calibration failures found on the way and does not claim the result is right.
- Intent: It is the one thing that makes a corner read as a corner from behind: without it, a car
  ahead slides toward the edge of the screen, gets smaller and vanishes off the side without ever
  turning to face you. It is also the system a kart racer needs to show a rival mid-drift.
- Note the limit it does not lift: **the road still cannot bend past 90 degrees on screen.** The
  cars can now look right through a corner the road only hints at. See the sweep-cap item.
- Done when: the angle thresholds are checked against screenshots through a real corner, and the
  three-quarter view is right at the angle it is actually used at.
