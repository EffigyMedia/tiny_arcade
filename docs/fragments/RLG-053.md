### RLG-053 — Five correctness defects in the Golden Era cabinets

- **Difficulty scaled with refresh rate in Swarm and Aegis (AUD-005).** Both wrote a per-frame
  probability as `0.014 * k * 60 * dt` with `k` already equal to `dt*60` - so the chance was
  proportional to **dt squared** and the per-second event rate proportional to frame rate. A 120 Hz
  phone, now ordinary, got roughly half the dive-bombs and half the incoming that a 60 Hz one did,
  and no two devices produced comparable scores. Both are linear in dt now, tuned to the rate the
  60 Hz numbers actually produced.
- **Pausing Ribbit while riding a pad could kill you (AUD-006).** Pads submerged on
  `performance.now()`, which keeps running while the shell holds the game paused, so a few seconds
  in the pause menu and a resume dropped the player into the water with no visible cause. **The
  shell promises a pause menu on every cabinet; one cabinet made using it dangerous.** Pads sink on
  an accumulated game clock now.
- **Burrow's fire-breathing drake was fully built and could never spawn (AUD-007).** The spawn line
  produced only wisps and grubs, so its telegraph, its three-cell flame with a kill check, its art,
  its breath sound and its 400-point case were all reachable from nothing. It arrives from level 3,
  which leaves the first two levels to teach digging and pumping before anything breathes fire.
- **Coil's turn buffer threw away the turn it promised to keep (AUD-008).** Both branches of the
  buffer assigned the same thing, so a fast double round a corner kept the SECOND input and
  discarded the first - the opposite of the behaviour its own CONTROLS page advertises, at the
  moment it matters most, since the tick shortens to 58 ms at length. It is genuinely two deep now.
- **Popshot could serve an unmatchable colour at the endgame (AUD-017).** With one colour left the
  pool fell back to three, contradicting the comment above it and the reason the function exists.
  One colour left now means one colour served.
- Verified: 18/18 cabinets plus the launcher check.
