### RLG-024 — Test a wrapped build on a real device
- Description: The product installs to a home screen today as a progressive web app. It has never
  been wrapped as a native application, and no wrapped build has been opened on a real device.
- Intent: **A webview is not a browser.** The two places it usually bites are audio autoplay and the
  safe-area insets, and both are load-bearing here: every sound is synthesized on a gesture, and
  every machine derives its layout from the shell's safe-area value.
- The wrapping decisions are already made and recorded in the design document section 7: Capacitor
  for mobile, Tauri for desktop, and Godot rejected because it exports to HTML rather than hosts it.
- Done when: a wrapped build runs on one real phone, makes sound on the first tap, and lays out
  correctly against the device's safe areas.
