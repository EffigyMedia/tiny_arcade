### RLG-007 — Pit road art
- Description: The pit lane is the last 9% of the lap and it works: below 60 mph the crew works,
  fuel goes in, and tires change. **It has no picture.** There is no lane, no wall, and no boxes.
- Intent: Fuel burn and tire wear are already real and already decide a race. This is the last piece
  of a mechanic that otherwise works, and its absence makes a working system read as unfinished.
- Done when: the pit lane is visibly a pit lane from the car, with a lane, a wall, and boxes.
