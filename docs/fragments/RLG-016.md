### RLG-016 — Bridges, now that the circuit shapes are settled
- Description: Crossovers, tunnels, and overpasses on a generated circuit.
- Intent: The circuit generator builds from a closed spline with zero crossings, and that constraint
  was the right one while the shapes were unsettled. They are settled now.
- The known cost, recorded before the standup so it is not rediscovered: **a crossover needs a
  second ribbon.** A pseudo-3D road draws one road surface, and a road that passes over another is
  two. This is the reason the item was queued rather than built.
- Done when: a generated circuit can cross itself, and the renderer draws both ribbons correctly.
