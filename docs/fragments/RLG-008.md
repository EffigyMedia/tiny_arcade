### RLG-008 — Connect the tire compounds to cornerG
- Description: `COMPOUNDS` defines grip at 1.10, 1.00, and 0.92 and **is not connected to anything**.
  The compound choice changes nothing about how the car corners.
- Intent: One line turns a cosmetic choice into a strategic one, and it is the piece that makes the
  pit stop a decision rather than a refuel.
- Depends on: [[RLG-002]]. While no corner forces a lift, a grip difference of 18% between compounds
  is not observable, so this lands after the corners bite.
- Done when: the compound the player chose multiplies `cornerG`, and a soft compound is measurably
  faster over one lap and measurably worse over five.
- **RULED 2026-08-23T23:59:13-04:00** — AMENDED 2026-08-23 - STILL NOT WIRED, AND THE CODE READS AS IF IT WERE. `cornerG()` in `road.js` is `CORNER_G_BASE / (BODY[optBody].grip * wetGrip())`. **The compound is not in it.** The confusing part is a comment at `games/sw/raceway.html:984` that reads *"...costs pace once COMPOUNDS.grip is finally wired into cornerG"* - a conditional written in the middle of a paragraph about wear tuning, and a fast reader takes it as a statement that the wiring happened. **It has not.** Compound still changes wear only: SOFT is x1.70 and HARD x0.62 on wear, and all three corner identically. Depends on nothing now - [[RLG-002]] is built, so the corners this would be measured against exist and a grip difference of 18% is finally observable. Done when: the chosen compound multiplies `cornerG`, and `drive-test.py` shows SOFT faster over one lap and worse over five.
