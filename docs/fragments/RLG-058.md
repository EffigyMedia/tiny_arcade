### RLG-058 — Milestones and handoffs are retired; the thread fragment replaces both
- **RULED 2026-08-25T00:26:03-04:00** — - **Ruling.** This project performs no milestone and writes no handoff. Both ceremonies are retired
  in this environment. Re-entry after a context clear is the thread fragment, `THR-001`, read with
  `<env-root>/Commands/thread.py show --store docs/fragments` and written only with that same tool.
  `docs/milestones/` is deleted and is not recreated.

- **Why the replacement is better, and it is not a matter of taste.** The difference is *when* the
  writing happens. A milestone and a handoff are both written **at the threshold** - composed by
  the session that is already out of room, from what that session can still remember. The thread is
  **maintained continuously**, so it is never written under that pressure. That is the whole reason
  a clear becomes cheap instead of expensive.

- **What this cost.** A handoff document was written and committed on 2026-08-25, at the owner's
  request, immediately before a clear, by a session that had not noticed the retirement. Its content
  was right; its form was obsolete. The owner corrected it, the content moved into the thread, and
  the document was deleted. The stale trigger had been sitting in `CLAUDE.md` since the standup,
  inherited from the project template and never checked against the process docs it claimed to
  summarize. **A trigger table that is copied rather than verified keeps a retired ceremony alive
  for as long as nobody reads it against its source.**

- **What is unchanged.** Audits stay, and `docs/audits/` stays. An audit is a piece of work with a
  finding, not a ceremony at a boundary. The changelog preamble now says its links are verified at
  every audit, which is what actually verifies them.

- **Where it is encoded.** `CLAUDE.md` - the trigger table loses two rows, gains a re-entry row that
  names the tool and the four origins, and gains a note below the table.
  `docs/core/Instruction_Changelog.md` - the dated amendment. `docs/fragments/THR-001.md` - the
  thread itself, carrying the successor project's brief in its unfinished lines.

- **One environment observation, not acted on from a project session.** `thread.py` titles the
  fragment from the writer, so this project's thread renders as "thread: EffigyMedia" rather than
  "thread: Tiny Arcade". Harmless while one owner keeps one thread per store; misleading the moment
  two stores are open beside each other.
