### RLG-002 — Corners that are slow enough to matter
- Description: A formula car in Raceway never drops below 182 mph. `drive-test.py` measured 194 mph
  sustained, zero braking, and 100% on-road for 30 seconds. Braking is 1% to 3% of a lap against a
  target near 15%. The tight radius exists, but only over a few hundred units.
- Intent: **This is the one that makes it a racing game or not.** A circuit that never forces a lift
  is a fast motorway with a lap counter. Every other item on the Raceway list decorates a race that
  the driving does not yet earn.
- The measuring stick already exists: `tools/drive-test.py` can report brake time as a share of the
  lap. Measure before the change and after it, and do not adjust a number by eye.
- What is known and settled, so it is not re-derived: curvature depends on radius and nothing else,
  `k = 1 / (R * CURVE_K)`. The corner cap past about 90 degrees is a renderer limit, not a taste
  one - past that angle the road leaves the frame. A previous pass found the scale constant, not the
  geometry, was the lever.
- Blocks: the sector delta and the qualifying lap are both worth less while every lap is flat out.
- Done when: `drive-test.py` reports brake time near 15% of a lap for a formula car on a generated
  circuit, and the car is not on the road 100% of the time by default.
- **RULED 2026-08-23T23:58:45-04:00** — BUILT, AND THIS ITEM WAS NEVER OPEN - IT WAS SEEDED FROM A STALE DOCUMENT. Verified in code on 2026-08-23. `games/sw/raceway.html` sets `CURVE_K = 0.000040` and clamps curvature to the tightest hairpin the league asked for (`kMax = 1 / (spec.hairpinR * CURVE_K)`). The frozen log's *THE CORNERS WORK* entry records the result: CUP 64s lap / slowest corner 99 mph / 6.9% braking; GT 75s / 102 mph / 10.1%; GP 88s / 123 mph / 7.7%, against 180 mph corners and 1-3% braking where it started. Crossings stayed at zero and lap times went from 68s to 88s. TWO THINGS IN THAT FIX ARE WORTH KEEPING IN MIND, because both cost real time to find. **The scale constant was the lever, not the geometry.** At Highway's 0.00028 a corner tight enough to slow a formula car had a radius of 320 units, crossed in 0.07 seconds - far too short to brake for, so the car was flung sideways rather than slowed. A SMALLER K lets a given radius bite while the corner stays physically long enough to last. `CURVE_K` is Raceway's own; Highway is untouched. **And the slowest point on every lap was a spline cusp, not a designed corner.** One sample wide, invisible at speed, minimum radius 265 where the hairpins asked for 3,800 - so the corners nobody designed governed the lap and the ones somebody did governed nothing. WHAT IS NOT VERIFIED HERE: `tools/drive-test.py` asserts peak speed and does not report brake time as a share of the lap or the slowest corner, so the numbers above are the frozen log's measurement rather than a repeatable one. That gap is now its own item.
